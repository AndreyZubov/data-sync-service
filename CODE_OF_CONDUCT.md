# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project welcomes contributions from everyone. Participants are expected to
treat each other with respect, regardless of experience level, background, or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith and ask questions before assuming a mistake was careless.
- Accept that the maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project's
  scope, and say so plainly rather than arguing it out at length.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory on this repository or by
contacting the maintainer through their GitHub profile
([@AndreyZubov](https://github.com/AndreyZubov)). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit, hide, or delete comments and may block
accounts that violate this policy.

This document is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
